# Joining on-call

1. Shadow two full primary shifts before taking one alone.
2. Complete the incident-response training in the LMS.
3. Ask your manager to add you to the PagerDuty schedule.

During a shift, follow [Incident response](../runbooks/sre-incident-response.md).
